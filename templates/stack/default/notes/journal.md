# journal

Running log of what actually happened in this space. Append, never rewrite.

Record the dead ends, not just the wins — the sentence "I assumed X, it was
actually Y, and it cost me 40 minutes" is the most valuable thing you can leave
for the next agent.

Use the `retro` skill. Promote recurring gotchas into `AGENTS.md`, repeated
procedures into `.agents/skills/`.
